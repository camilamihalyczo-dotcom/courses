INDUSTRY GLOSSARY: CORE VARIABLES

Class 02 • Cross-Team Actions, Incident Response & Standup Flow

[Student Workbook]
DM [dee-em]
Action Verb

To send a brief direct message on Teams or chat.

> Example

"I will DM DevOps right after this call."

REACH OUT TO [RIICH aut tu]
Phrasal Verb

To contact a person or external team for coordination or help.

> Example

"Have you reached out to the infrastructure team yet?"

TAKE CARE OF [TEIK ker ov]
Verb Phrase

To assume full responsibility for handling or resolving a task.

> Example

"Don't worry about the query error, I will take care of it."

ON TRACK [on TRAK]
Idiom / Status

Proceeding according to schedule; meeting the planned deadlines.

> Example

"Everything is on track. No critical blockers today."

FALL BEHIND [FOL bi-JAIND]
Phrasal Verb

To fail to keep up with the sprint pace or project schedule.

> Example

"We are falling behind schedule due to server downtime."

HEADS UP [hedz-AP]
Noun / Alert

An advance warning or informal alert about a future change.

> Example

"Just a quick heads up: the database will restart at 3 PM."

LOGS [logz]
Technical Noun

Official time-stamped records of system operations or errors.

> Example

"I will inspect the container logs to find the root cause."

CLUSTER [KLAS-ter]
Infrastructure

A group of interconnected machines/servers working as a single system.

> Example

"The model took two hours to train on the GPU cluster."

MEMORY LEAK [MEM-ri liik]
Bug / Incident

A failure in a program to release allocated memory, degrading performance.

> Example

"We noticed a small memory leak in the data loader loop."

UNDERSTOOD [an-der-STUD]
Communication

Clear, professional confirmation that an instruction or status was received.

> Example

"Understood. I will keep you posted on our progress."

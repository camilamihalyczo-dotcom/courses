Class 01 • The Daily Agile Standup & Action Verbs

[Student Workbook]
PIPELINE [PAIP-lain]
Noun

Automated workflow that moves and transforms data from source to destination.

> Standup Example

"The data pipeline runs every morning at 6 AM."

BOTTLENECK [BOT-l-nek]
Noun

A point of congestion or impediment that slows down the whole system.

> Standup Example

"Data extraction is our main bottleneck right now."

BLOCKER [BLOK-er]
Noun

An impediment that stops you from making progress on your current task.

> Standup Example

"I have no blockers today; everything is on track."

DEPLOY [di-PLOI]
Action Verb (Past: Deployed)

To release and run a model or script in a production environment.

> Standup Example

"Yesterday, we deployed the baseline model to staging."

TUNE [TIUN]
Action Verb (Cont: Tuning)

To adjust parameters or hyperparameters to optimize performance.

> Standup Example

"Today, I am tuning hyperparameters for better accuracy."

FEATURE [FII-cher]
Noun

An individual measurable property or variable used as input for a model.

> Standup Example

"We engineered three new features from customer logs."

STANDUP [STAND-ap]
Noun

A daily short status meeting (10–15 mins) to align team priorities.

> Standup Example

"Let's discuss this right after the morning standup."

ETL [ii-tii-el]
Acronym / Process

Extract, Transform, Load: consolidating data from multiple sources.

> Standup Example

"The ETL job failed last night due to memory limits."

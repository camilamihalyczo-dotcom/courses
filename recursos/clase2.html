> Technical Bridge

The Conversation Loop (Handling Interruptions)

⚙️ Framework

In international tech standups, the Tech Lead interrupts with unexpected questions. Use this 3-step bridge:

⏱️ STEP 1: TIME BUYER

Buy thinking time without awkward silences.

"Good question. Well, basically..."
"Sure, let me verify that on my screen..."
📊 STEP 2: DIRECT ANSWER

State clear facts, runtimes, or system metrics.

"It took around two hours..."
"We noticed a small memory leak..."
⚡ STEP 3: NEXT ACTION

State immediate focus in Present Continuous.

"I am inspecting the logs right now."
"I am DMing DevOps on Teams after this call."

> Survival Protocols

Clarification Formulas (Never Say "What?")

✨ Professional Polish
🔄 1. RETRY (Repetition)

When speech is too fast or audio cuts out:

"Could you repeat that, please?"
"Sorry, could you say that again?"

🔍 2. DEBUG (Scope)

To clarify ambiguous technical questions:

"Do you mean on staging or in production?"
"Are you referring to latency or accuracy?"

✅ 3. ACKNOWLEDGE (Action)

To confirm immediate ownership:

"Understood. I am taking care of it now."
"Got it. I am updating the team on Teams."

💡 Pro Tip (Future with -ING): In tech standups, using Present Continuous ("I am taking care of it now" / "I am DMing DevOps right after this call") sounds much more agile, natural, and immediate, but we are still talking about future actions. Avoid the simple future tense ("I will take care of it") in these scenarios, as it can sound less proactive.

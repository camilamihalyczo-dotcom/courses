Stage 1 // 05 Min

Spontaneous Warm-up: The Coffee Chat

The Conversation Loop
Trigger 1: Energy & Day Check

"Morning Angeles! How is your day going so far? Are you having a busy week?"

👉 Answer with detail: Estado general + 1 tarea clave.

Trigger 2: Weekend & Weather

"Did you have a relaxing weekend? How is the weather over there today?"

👉 Answer with detail: Descanso / aire libre + temperatura.

Professional Bounce-Backs:
  • • "How about yourself?" (La más formal)
  • • "How are things on your side / on your end?"
  • • "How is your week treating you so far?"
  • • "What about you? How is your day going?"
  • • "Did you have a good [weekend/morning] too?"
The Golden Rule: Never answer with just 1 word NO MONOSÍLABOS

Evitá decir solo "Fine" o "Good". Agregá siempre: (1) Un detalle corto + (2) Devolución de pregunta:

❌ "Fine." / "Good."
✅ "Pretty good! A bit busy with logistics reports, but all under control. How about yourself?"
❌ "Tired."
✅ "A little tired today, to be honest! I need another coffee. How are things on your side?"
❌ "Normal." / "Same."
✅ "Same as usual! Checking emails and organizing my week. How is your week treating you so far?"
❌ "Good weekend."
✅ "It was very relaxing! I spent Sunday outdoors and rested. Did you have a good weekend too?"

Roleplay 1 // 12 Min

Daily Standup: Routines vs. Projects (-ING)

Simulation Drill
Teacher: David (Operations Manager)

"Morning Angeles, could you please give us a quick status update on your priorities?"

Student: Angeles (Logistics Coordinator)

Tu misión oral: Mencionar 1 responsabilidad habitual de tu rutina y 2 tareas en progreso esta semana.

Useful Phrases (Usá estas fórmulas):
Routine: "Every morning, I check incoming correspondence and invoices."
Preposition: "I am responsible for managing supplier accounts and databases."
In Progress: "Currently / This week, I am working on the export consignment notes."
In Progress: "At the moment, I am contacting carriers to confirm delivery dates."

Roleplay 2 // 12 Min

Handling Delays: Apologies & Commitments with WILL

Business Friction
Teacher: Mr. Davis (Internal Stakeholder)

"Angeles, where is the agricultural logistics report? We needed it on Wednesday."

Student: Angeles (Commercialization Consultant)

Tu misión oral: Pedir disculpas diplomáticas, explicar que el proveedor retrasó los datos (data, no date) y comprometerte a entregarlo mañana antes de EOD con WILL.

Useful Phrases (Manejo de demoras):
Formal Apology: "Please accept my sincere apologies for the delay."
The Blocker: "Unfortunately, I was waiting for data from the supplier..."
Firm Commitment: "However, I will send the updated report tomorrow before EOD."
Appreciation: "Thank you very much for your patience and understanding."

Roleplay 3 // 12 Min

Slack Audio Call: Sick Leave & Workload Handover

Emergency Call
Teacher: Carlos (Team Leader)

"Hi Angeles! I saw your Slack notification. Is everything okay?"

Student: Angeles (Team Member)

Tu misión oral: Explicar que tenés dolor de cabeza/migraña usando under the weather, que te tomás el día de reposo y que mañana te ponés al día.

Useful Phrases (Salud y Ausencia):
Health Status: "I woke up feeling a bit under the weather with a headache."
The Action: "So, I decided to take a sick day today to rest."
Backup: "For any urgent matters regarding consignment notes, Mariana can assist."
Catch up: "I will catch up on all pending emails tomorrow morning."

Drill // 05 Min

Rapid-Fire: Spot the Error & Correct It Out Loud

Speed Drill

Leé cada frase en silencio, detectá el error de transferencia y decila en voz alta de la forma correcta:

1. Compromiso futuro

❌ "I send the report to the supplier tomorrow."

👉 Decilo bien: "I ____________ the report tomorrow."

2. Regla de Preposición

❌ "I am responsible of organize the invoices."

👉 Decilo bien: "I am responsible ____________ ____________ the invoices."

3. Cierre de Email

❌ "I look forward to receive your feedback."

👉 Decilo bien: "I look forward to ____________ your feedback."

4. Expresión de Salud

❌ "I am bad today, I have bad head."

👉 Decilo bien: "I am feeling ____________ ____________ ____________ today."

Lexicon // Reference

Key Lexical Chunks (Clases 1 a 3)

Core Vocabulary
Actions in Progress

Currently + -ing

"Currently, I am working on export consignment notes."

Duty & Preposition

Responsible for + -ing

"I am responsible for coordinating logistics and invoices."

Future Commitment

I will + verb base

"I will send the confirmed report before EOD tomorrow."

Health Status

Feeling under the weather

"I am feeling under the weather due to a mild headache."

Short-Term Absence

Take a sick day

"I will take a sick day today and catch up tomorrow."

Email Closing

Look forward to + -ing

"I look forward to receiving your confirmation soon."

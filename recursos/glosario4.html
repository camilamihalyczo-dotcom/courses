OFFICIAL INTEGRATION GLOSSARY

Glosario oficial de integración: Operaciones, fórmulas laborales y comunicación cotidiana

Nivel A2 — Review Lab
1. Workplace Operations & Logistics (Entorno Laboral y Trámites)
Término / Fórmula Categoría Definición & Ejemplo de Uso Significado / Traducción
Consignment note Noun / Logistics Official document accompanying a shipment of goods.
e.g. "I am organizing the export consignment notes."		 Nota de consignación / Guía de despacho
Supplier data Collocation Information/prices provided by a vendor (data = datos, not date).
e.g. "I was waiting for supplier data to finish the report."		 Datos / información del proveedor
Responsible for (+ -ing) Grammar Rule Fixed rule: Stating duties with preposition + gerund.
e.g. "I am responsible for verifying freight receipts."		 Responsable de (+ acción en -ing)
Due to / Slight delay Formal Phrase Diplomatic way to introduce the cause of a postponement.
e.g. "There is a slight delay due to pending confirmations."		 Debido a / Leve demora o retraso
Firm commitment with WILL Grammar Rule Rule: Using will to give deadlines and promises (not Present).
e.g. "I will send the file before EOD tomorrow."		 Compromiso futuro ("Enviaré...", "Haré...")
2. Professional Well-being & Handover (Salud y Ausencias en Slack)
Término / Fórmula Categoría Definición & Ejemplo de Uso Significado / Traducción
Under the weather Corporate Idiom Diplomatic phrase meaning slightly sick, unwell, or exhausted.
e.g. "I woke up feeling a bit under the weather today."		 Con malestar / No sentirse del todo bien
Take a sick day Collocation To take an excused absence day from work due to illness.
e.g. "I will take a sick day today to rest."		 Tomarse el día por enfermedad / reposo
Catch up on Phrasal Verb To complete pending work that accumulated while away.
e.g. "I will catch up on pending emails tomorrow morning."		 Ponerse al día con tareas o correos
Handover / Urgent backup Workplace Duty Delegating urgent responsibilities to a colleague during absence.
e.g. "For urgent matters, Mariana can assist."		 Traspaso de tareas / Contacto de respaldo
3. Casual Small Talk, Habits & Preferences (Rutinas, Gustos y Descanso)
Expresión / Pregunta Categoría Cuándo y Cómo Usarlo Significado / Función
The Conversation Loop Strategy Technique: Give a brief answer and immediately bounce back.
e.g. "It's going well, thanks! And how about you?"		 Ciclo de respuesta + devolución de pregunta
I usually / On weekends... Present Simple Used to describe typical habits and weekend routines.
e.g. "On weekends, I usually disconnect and go for a walk."		 Por lo general... / Los fines de semana...
I prefer / I enjoy (+ -ing) Preference Verbs of preference followed by noun or action in -ing.
e.g. "I prefer having coffee before checking messages."		 Prefiero... / Disfruto de (+ acción)
At the moment / Currently watching Present Continuous Talking about temporary leisure projects, series or books.
e.g. "At the moment, I am watching a crime thriller series."		 En este momento estoy viendo/haciendo...
Stare at screens for too long Work-life Idiom Common cause of digital fatigue or eye strain at office work.
e.g. "My head hurts from staring at the screen for too long."		 Pasar demasiadas horas frente a la pantalla
How about yourself? Bounce-Back Polite, formal alternative to "And you?" for clients and managers.
e.g. "Pretty good, thanks! How about yourself?"		 ¿Y usted? / ¿Y vos? (devolución formal)
How are things on your end? Remote Work Asking about workload or progress in another office/location.
e.g. "All good here! How are things on your end?"		 ¿Cómo vienen las cosas por tu lado?
How is your week treating you? Small Talk Friendly conversational opener used mid-week before business.
e.g. "Busy week! How is your week treating you so far?"		 ¿Cómo te viene tratando la semana?
Stay at home vs. Go out Daily Habit Contrasting quiet weekend recovery with social activities.
e.g. "I usually prefer staying at home on Sundays."		 Quedarse en casa vs. Salir
Log off Phrasal Verb To disconnect from digital tools or finish the workday.
e.g. "Please log off early and take a sick day to rest."		 Desconectarse / Cerrar sesión
